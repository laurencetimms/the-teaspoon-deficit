## Trade economy

Trading with the Quorate is currently a free pass to notions: the price stays at 2 ledgers while the payout grows with the notions store. Fix it with a small market, not rising prices.

1. **Neighbours with memories.** *(built, 19 Nov)* Each neighbour has stock and appetite.
   - Selling them a lot of one thing lowers what they will pay for it; buying a lot raises their price until they restock. Both drift back over time.
   - The Quorate's notions wear out too ("They have told you this already").
   - Seasons shift demand (the Choir wants tea at Aphelion), so trading becomes about timing: whom, and when.
2. **The Mind trades** (stage two). Once prices move, the Mind can work the market:
   - a Haggle task for spare thought;
   - a Broker subsystem that reads the market oddly (a Pendulum-of-Maybe broker sees swings that are not there);
   - a trade-desk duty team that learns by trial and error, and sometimes buys high or sells the ship's last spoons.
3. **The Quorate's auditor.** *(built, 19 Nov)* A story chain: the Quorate notice the arbitrage and send an auditor, who may stay on as a character.

Measure it with the harness: the share of each life's notions that come from trade.

Decided against: prices that simply rise with each trade, like hull buildings.
